      Private Treaty Listing

      The Community and Workers of Jamaica Co-operative Credit Union Limited (C&WJCCUL) invites written offers from interested persons wishing to purchase any of the properties being sold under the Powers of Sale contained in a mortgage (see link below).

      All offers and enquiries should be directed to the Debt Recovery Unit, located at 51 Half Way Tree Road, Kingston 10, or submitted via email to privatetreatylisting@cwjcu.com or info@cwjcu.com.

      For queries or to speak with the Debt Recovery Manager, please call 876‑936‑3800.

Frequently asked questions

What is a repossessed car or house in Jamaica?

A repossessed car or house is a vehicle or property that a Jamaican bank or credit union has taken back because the previous owner could not keep up the loan payments. The lender then sells it — often by private treaty or auction — to recover the money owed, which is why these listings are frequently priced below the open market.

How do I buy a repossessed vehicle or property in Jamaica?

Find the listing you want, click Visit site to open the bank or credit union's own page, then contact that institution directly to arrange a viewing, make an offer, or place a bid. Always confirm the price, condition and title details with the lender before you buy.

Which banks and credit unions are included?

We gather listings from 12 sources, including NCB (National Commercial Bank), JMMB, Sagicor, JN Bank, First Global Bank, Scotiabank, JPS & Employees Co-operative Credit Union and more.

Is Repossessed Jamaica free to use?

Yes. Repossessed Jamaica is completely free. It simply collects the repossessed house and car lists that Jamaican banks already publish and puts them in one searchable place.

Always double-check details with the bank before you buy. ❤️
